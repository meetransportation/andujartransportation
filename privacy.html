Section 01

Introduction

Andujar Transportation Inc. ("Company," "we," "our," or "us") provides interstate freight and vehicle transportation services within the United States.

We coordinate transportation services through third-party logistics platforms where customers post loads and/or vehicles for pickup and delivery. To complete these services, we communicate directly with customers via phone and SMS text messaging to obtain approvals, confirm delivery addresses, schedule pickup and delivery times, and provide service updates.

Section 02

Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of personal information:

  • Name and company name
  • Mobile and business phone numbers
  • Email address
  • Pickup and delivery addresses
  • Vehicle or freight details
  • Billing or invoicing information
  • SMS and communication records
Section 03

How We Use Information

We use collected information strictly for operational and business purposes, including:

  • Coordinating freight pickup and delivery
  • Obtaining shipment approvals
  • Confirming availability and scheduling
  • Providing dispatch and status updates
  • Responding to inquiries
  • Issuing invoices and documentation
  • Maintaining regulatory compliance

We do not sell or rent personal information or mobile numbers to third parties for marketing purposes.

Section 04

Information Sharing

We may share information only when necessary to:

  • Complete transportation services
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Work with trusted service providers (e.g., dispatch platforms, accounting providers, and our VoIP provider)

All service providers are required to maintain confidentiality.

Section 05

Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect personal information. However, no transmission method is completely secure.

Section 06

Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill services, comply with regulations, resolve disputes, and maintain business records.

📱 SMS Messaging Terms — A2P 10DLC Compliant

The following sections (8–15) govern our SMS text messaging practices in compliance with U.S. carrier A2P 10DLC requirements, the TCPA, and CTIA Messaging Principles.

Section 08

Purpose of SMS Communications

We use SMS messaging strictly for transactional and operational communications related to freight and vehicle transportation services, including:

  • Pickup confirmations
  • Delivery scheduling
  • Address verification
  • Dispatch coordination
  • Status updates
  • Approval requests
  • Issue resolution

We do not send unsolicited marketing messages.

Section 09

Consent (Opt-In)

By providing your mobile number to Andujar Transportation Inc., whether directly or through a third-party logistics platform, you consent to receive SMS messages related to transportation services.

Consent may be obtained via direct communication, digital platforms, written authorization, or verbal agreement.

Message frequency varies based on shipment activity.

Section 10

Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile carrier plan.

Section 11
